# Writing Sample Template for Voice Calibration

Use this template to provide samples of your authentic writing to Universal Humanizer. The skill analyzes your sentence rhythm, vocabulary, paragraph structure, and stylistic quirks to match your natural voice.

---

## Instructions for Best Results

1. **Original Prose Only:** Provide 2 to 4 paragraphs that you personally wrote from scratch without AI assistance (e.g., a blog post, technical explanation, email, or essay).
2. **Grammar & Quirks:** Do not clean up your natural quirks. If you frequently use em dashes, short one-sentence paragraphs, rhetorical questions, or informal parentheticals, leave them intact. The humanizer will emulate your actual rhythm.
3. **Audience Specification:** State who you are writing for (engineers, managers, general public, students).

---

## Prompt Template

Copy and paste the following block into your agent chat:

```text
/humanizer

### Target Audience & Context
- Audience: [e.g., Senior backend engineers / Product managers / Blog readers]
- Context: [e.g., Internal technical proposal / Public release announcement]

### My Writing Sample (for voice calibration)
[Paste 2-4 paragraphs of your own original writing here]

### Text to Humanize
[Paste the AI-generated draft you want to rewrite here]
```
